In the battle against an identity crisis—and against all the parasites feeding
on it—Lea Karin roams into battle using her cardboard sword and clay horse.

Long delayed, shifted around, and put on hold, the concept of Lea Karin was born in a child's bedroom and grew up in various practice rooms, only to finally step out into the light during her late teens. Battles against windmills, rock 'n' roll and cartoons — Lea Karin’s debut release is here.
