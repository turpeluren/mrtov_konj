Vagina Corporation, a band hailing from the vibrant music scene of Skopje, North Macedonia, has been making waves with their unique sound—a fusion of Kraut-Rock, Americana, and Psychedelia, affectionately known as KRAP. Their music, driven by the angst of our times and inspired by the concrete jungle of Skopje, is a powerful experience that aims to immerse the audience in a mad, angry yet hopeful dance—a dance that beckons the final rain.
