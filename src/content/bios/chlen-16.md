Mice and worms squeal through the worn parquet and the ceiling is always out of reach. The lamp burns pale and green and the doors slowly creak and scratch. Chlen 16 live here. In fact, we are here too, all of us. The old middle school friends and the lunatic who jerks off at the bus stop. In this house, change happens slowly, only when someone manically screams. And from that mania and chaos, like a breathless drowning man barely saving himself, the voice of reason emerges. Reason without comforting dissuasions, without decorative ribbons, and without censorship.
