Actually, Lufthansa from Macedonia would have a fourth guitarist, but he is not there today. But three are also more than enough to release fine, playful post-punk songs with an occasional rough twist to the packed auditorium of the Der AA Theater. Dressed in smart army jackets from a bygone era, the band also shows traditional guitar influences. In addition, the repetitive nature of the songs is also reminiscent of The Fall at times. The singing in Macedonian adds an extra mysterious dimension to Lufthansa's varied sound. The formation's strong point is the exploitation of loud-soft dynamics in pleasantly rattling songs.
