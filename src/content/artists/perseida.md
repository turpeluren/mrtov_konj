Beneath thick layers of guitars and seismic drums and bass emerges the voice of Perseida—a Macedonian shoegaze band formed in 2025.

Their melodies drift between dreams and reality, where chaos and distortion coalesce into a harmonious sound. Much like watching a meteor shower from an open field, Perseida—with each song acting as a distinct star within a constellation—sings of a different emotion.
