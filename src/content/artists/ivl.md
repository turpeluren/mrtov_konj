IVL is a noise trio from Skopje, made up of Vasil Jordanov, Lila Mirchevska and Iva Damjanovski.

They formed at the start of 2025 as a Throbbing Gristle tribute band and a continuation of Vasil's project ШУМ (SHUM, "Noise"). So far they have played several shows, including the festival "Sepak se vrti" ("Still, It Turns") in Kumanovo and Vasil Fest in Bukva.

The album "...и за работа и за дружење" ("...for work and for company") came out in December 2025. It had a listening and playing launch at Kinoteka, as a live soundtrack over a screening of archive films from Kinoteka's own archive. The event was part of Kinoteka's program.

"The VIL to LIV!"\
-Lila
